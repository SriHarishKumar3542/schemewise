About SchemeWise

SchemeWise is a social-benefit platform designed to make public health information easier to discover and understand. It helps users explore health schemes, check potential eligibility, understand application procedures and find upcoming health camps in one place.

Our Vision

To make access to health-support information simpler, clearer and more accessible through technology.

Our Approach

SchemeWise focuses on guidance and information rather than collecting unnecessary personal data or replacing official healthcare services.
